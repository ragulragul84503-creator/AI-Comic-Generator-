THE AI PIPELINE

How It Works

Transforming your brief imagination into an illustrated, dialogue-rich comic book is as simple as 4 steps.

01

Describe Your Idea

Tell ComicCraft what story you want to create, name your protagonist, and select your setting.

02

AI Builds Your Story

AI analyzes your prompt and crafts the 5-panel narrative outline, dramatic voiceover captions, and character dialogue.

03

AI Creates Your Artwork

Each panel receives a tailored comic illustration matching your chosen art style—from anime to retro pixel art.

04

Download Your Comic

Review panels, customize text if desired, and export your finished comic as a print-ready professional PDF.

ORIGINAL ARCHITECTURE

AI Meets Storytelling

ComicCraft combines generative AI storytelling and visual image synthesis to empower anyone to craft complete, illustrated multi-panel comics without requiring professional drawing skills.

The production architecture integrates a high-performance FastAPI server with Gemini models for structured outlines, detailed character dialogue, Stable Diffusion for comic panels, and FPDF for document compilation.

⚡ FastAPI Backend 🎨 HTML / CSS / Jinja2 🧠 Gemini Flash & Pro 🖼️ Stable Diffusion SDXL 📄 FPDF Engine
